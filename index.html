Your business stays your business

This tool was built the Lesko Help way: free, private, and on your side.

Everything stays on your device

Your answers and your results are saved only in this browser, on this device — like notes on your own refrigerator. Nothing is sent to us, to Lesko Help, or to anyone else. There is no account, no email, no tracking of what you pick.

Never type these anywhere in this tool

Your Social Security number, bank or card numbers, or passwords. This tool never asks for them — and neither does any honest government program at the "first phone call" stage. Anyone who demands your SSN plus a fee before they'll "release your grant" is a scammer.

Good to know
  • Clearing your browser data, or switching devices or browsers, means your saved results won't follow you — print them or copy them somewhere safe if you want a backup.
  • The links in your results go to official government and nonprofit websites. Once you leave this page, their privacy rules apply.
  • This tool is education and research help from the Lesko Help community — it can show you where the programs are, but only the agency giving out money can decide if you qualify.
