Your business stays your business

This tool was built the Lesko Help way: private, simple, and on your side.

Everything stays on your device

Your answers and your list are saved only in this browser, on this device — like notes on your own refrigerator. Nothing is sent to us, to Lesko Help, or to anyone else. There is no account, no email, no tracking of what you pick.

Never type these anywhere in this tool

Your Social Security number, bank or card numbers, or passwords. This tool never asks for them — and neither does any honest government program at the "first phone call" stage. Anyone who demands your SSN plus a fee before they'll "release your grant" is a scammer.

Good to know
  • Clearing your browser data, or switching devices or browsers, means your saved list won't follow you — print it or copy it somewhere safe if you want a backup.
  • The links in your list go back into the Lesko Help community — its guides, channels and classes. Once you leave this page, that site's rules apply.
  • This tool does one thing: it helps you name your problems and put them in order. The community's guides, researchers and classes take each problem from there.
